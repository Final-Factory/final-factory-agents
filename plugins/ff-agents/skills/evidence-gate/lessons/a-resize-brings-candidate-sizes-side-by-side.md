# A resize brings candidate sizes side by side, not one new size

**Rule.** When the request is "too big", "too small" or any size or spacing change, the stills for the person show 2 or 3
candidate sizes side by side in one image: for example the current size, a middle size and the other bound. One image per
zoom or resolution the complaint names, from a built game, each candidate labelled with its measured size (px, or a
multiple of the structure it marks). Where a visible reference exists (a neighbouring structure's top, an icon, a sibling
panel), match it and say what you matched; never guess a multiplier.

**Why.** w3243 (violet circuit marks), Ben, 2026-11-06: "the pink circuit overlay is too big when zoomed out, it crowds out
stuff". The worker brought one new size, a 4 px floor. Ben: "It's too small now". The next try was one 5 px size (shown
beside master and the 4 px try, but all three were the same kind of change, a pad size). Ben: "Still too small; make the
lines thicker but keep the pads off the structures." Three rounds, each ending in a bound Ben had to find by rejecting.
The right answer was in another dimension (line thickness, pad placement) that no single-size try could show. w2195
(Shield Generator): a size was guessed as a multiplier; Ben: "i want the spire thing to start at the level of the cargo
hold tops there", a visible reference the worker could have matched.

**How to apply.**
- A complaint that names a direction ("too big") has a bound on each side: master's size is one, the over-correction
  is the other. Show both and a middle, so one reply picks.
- If the mark has several parts (pad, line, glow), vary the part the person named and also offer one candidate that
  varies another part, so a "not that part" answer costs no round.
- Take every zoom or resolution the person named (zoomed out, mid, close; 1920x1080, Deck), side by side in one still
  each; a candidate good at one zoom and bad at another is the usual cause of the next round.
- Label each candidate with the measured size from the build, not the number you set; a floor or clamp can change it.
- The ask card for the person previews that one still per zoom, not separate stills they must compare in their head.
- The `ui-critic` flags a size-change pack that shows a single candidate (its item 7d). That is a role instruction, not a
  script: no tool counts candidates in a still, so the checklist line and the critic are the whole check.
